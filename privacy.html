Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

Summary

Luster is built from the ground up to respect your privacy. All your vehicles, detailing sessions, before/after photos, inventory, reminders, estimates, and invoices stay exclusively on your device. We do not track you, run analytics, show advertising, or operate remote servers that collect your personal information.

1. What Data Stays on Your Device

Everything you enter into Luster is stored locally using Apple's native SwiftData framework:

  • Vehicle Profiles: Make, model, year, nickname, odometer, VIN, license plate, color, and photos.
  • Detailing Sessions: Date, time, duration, services performed, products used, weather, and notes.
  • Before & After Photos: High-resolution images are kept strictly in the app's local document sandbox.
  • Product Inventory: Chemical names, sizes, purchase prices, and remaining bottle percentages.
  • Client Invoices & Estimates: Client names, line items, pricing, deposits, and payments.

2. System Permissions We Request

  • Camera: Used solely when you choose to photograph a vehicle or capture a before/after shot with the alignment camera. No images are transmitted.
  • Photo Library: Used only when you manually select a vehicle profile picture or detailing photo from your library.
  • Face ID / Biometrics: Optional. Used strictly by the iOS Secure Enclave to unlock the app if you enable App Lock in Settings. Luster never receives your biometric data.
  • Notifications: Used exclusively to trigger on-device maintenance reminders (e.g., "Wax again in 60 days").
  • Calendars: When adding a reminder to your Apple Calendar, the system event edit sheet is presented. Luster cannot read or search your existing calendar events.

3. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases and subscriptions for Luster Plus are processed securely by Apple through StoreKit. Luster does not collect or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment credentials.

4. Exporting & Backups

You can create full encrypted .zip backups of your garage, complete with full-resolution photos, or export CSV and JSON summaries. These files are saved only where you choose (such as Files, AirDrop, or external drives).

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please visit our Support Page or email us at: support@lusterapp.com.

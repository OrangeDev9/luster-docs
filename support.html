Frequently Asked Questions

How do I transfer my data to a new iPhone or iPad?

Luster features an integrated offline backup system. In Settings > Your Data, tap Back Up Everything (.zip) and save the file to iCloud Drive or AirDrop it to your new device. On your new device, open Luster Settings and tap Restore from Backup.

How do I restore my Luster Plus purchase?

If you reinstalled the app or set up a new device with the same Apple ID, go to Settings > Luster Plus and tap Restore Purchases. StoreKit will immediately verify your active entitlement.

How does Treatment Protection tracking work?

Luster calculates protection degradation based on automotive industry lifespans (Waxes: 60 days, Sealants: 90 days, Ceramic Coatings: 365+ days). Whenever you log a detailing session, the protection timer automatically resets with live visual wear-off gauges on the vehicle dashboard.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad > tap your Name / Apple ID > tap Subscriptions > select Luster Plus > tap Cancel Subscription.
